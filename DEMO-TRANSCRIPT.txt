========================================================================
GSD - DEMO VOICEOVER TRANSCRIPT (~4 min)
========================================================================

Read-aloud script for a screen-recorded demo of GSD (Getting Stuff Done).
Narration is plain text; [ ON SCREEN: ... ] cues are what to do while
talking. Value-first: this focuses on WHY GSD is different, not on basic
clicks. ~4 minutes at a natural pace.
Demo data = Michael Scott's list (today = Aug 17, 2026).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 1 - THE PROBLEM (~30s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: A long, flat checklist - cluttered notes / sticky notes. ]

Every to-do list works for about a week. Then it grows into a flat wall of
items where "call Mom" sits right next to "prep the quarterly numbers."
Everything looks equally urgent, which means nothing does.

The hard part was never remembering WHAT to do - it's deciding WHAT TO DO
NEXT, and knowing what you can safely ignore right now. A plain list can't
answer that. GSD is built to.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 2 - WHAT GSD IS (~35s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Open gsd.html; land on the main task list. ]

GSD - Getting Stuff Done - is built on the Getting Things Done framework:
get everything out of your head into a trusted system, then choose your next
action by an item's ATTRIBUTES - priority, urgency, size, category - instead
of by whatever's shouting loudest.

And it's deliberately tiny: ONE HTML file, no install, no account, no
server, works offline. This demo list belongs to Michael Scott - let's use
it to get him sorted.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 3 - THE WHOLE LIST, READABLE AT A GLANCE (~40s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Scroll the list slowly; let the colors do the talking. ]

Here's the first payoff. You can READ this list without reading it.

The colored border and dot are PRIORITY - red High, orange Medium, green
Low. The date chip is URGENCY: "Prep quarterly numbers" is red because it's
already overdue; "Call Mom" is amber because it's due today. The solid pill
is the CATEGORY, the grey badge is SIZE - effort at a glance.

So before doing anything, Michael already knows the red, overdue work items
are where his attention belongs - and the "Update LinkedIn to World's Best
Boss" task can wait.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 4 - SMART SORT: WHAT TO DO NEXT (~45s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Sort dropdown -> "Smart (auto)". Point at the new top of the
list. ]

That instinct is exactly what SMART sort automates - and it's the heart of
the app.

Instead of sorting on one field, it scores every task by blending urgency,
priority, and a quick-win boost for smaller tasks, then floats the
important-and-urgent ones to the top. Watch: the overdue quarterly numbers
and the past-due car registration rise up here; the Dundie Awards and
calling Mom sit right behind; and the low-priority, no-deadline stuff sinks
to the bottom on its own.

That's the whole promise - you open the app and the top of the list IS your
answer to "what now?" No triage required.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 5 - AWAITING: GET "NOT MY MOVE RIGHT NOW" OUT OF THE WAY (~40s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Scroll to the Awaiting section; point at the two purple
hourglass tasks. ]

The other half of staying focused is hiding what you CAN'T act on. That's
AWAITING - pure GTD "waiting-on."

Michael's waiting on a promo rate to book Jamaica, and on HR to transfer
Toby. Those don't belong in his active list, so they live down here, out of
his face.

[ ON SCREEN: On an active task, click the hourglass to defer with a note +
bring-back date. ]

Deferring anything takes one click - you note what you're waiting on and
when to resurface it. And the key part: on that date it AUTOMATICALLY
returns to the active list. You never have to remember to check back - which
is the entire point of a trusted system.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 6 - FRICTIONLESS CAPTURE (~25s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Quickly: + to add, click a row to edit/complete/delete, hover
for the duplicate icon. Fast montage. ]

Getting tasks IN is just as low-friction. The plus button captures a new
task with all its fields; clicking any row edits, completes, or deletes it;
hovering duplicates a repeat. And search and filters shrink a long list in a
keystroke. None of it needs explaining - it just stays out of your way so
the framework can do the work.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 7 - YOUR DATA, YOUR FILES (~30s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Open the menu; point at auto-save status, Switch folder, Export
backup (.zip). ]

One more thing that matters: you OWN your data. GSD stores your tasks as
plain JSON files in a folder you pick - that folder IS the database, and
every change auto-saves there instantly. No cloud, no lock-in.

You can back up to a zip in one click, move the folder to another machine,
even diff it in git. It's your list, in your files, forever.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 8 - WRAP-UP (~20s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Return to the list with Smart sort on. ]

So - one offline file, your data, and a list that TELLS you what to do next:
priority and urgency visible at a glance, Smart sort surfacing what matters,
and everything you're just waiting on quietly out of the way until it's
actionable.

Get it out of your head. Let GSD tell you what's next. That's getting stuff
done.

[ ON SCREEN: Fade out. ]
