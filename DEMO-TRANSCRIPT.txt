========================================================================
GSD - DEMO VOICEOVER TRANSCRIPT (~3.5 min)
========================================================================

Read-aloud script for a screen-recorded demo of GSD (Getting Stuff Done).
Narration is plain text; [ ON SCREEN: ... ] cues are what to do while
talking. Feature-first: short setup, then straight into what makes GSD
different.
Demo data = Michael Scott's list (today = Aug 29, 2026).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 1 - THE PROBLEM & WHAT GSD IS (~20s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Open gsd.html; land on the main task list. ]

Every to-do list becomes a flat wall where "call Mom" sits next to "prep
the quarterly numbers" - everything looks urgent, so nothing is. GSD fixes
that.

Built on the Getting Things Done framework, it's ONE offline HTML file - no
install, no account, no server - that picks your next action by an item's
ATTRIBUTES instead of by whatever's shouting loudest. This is Michael
Scott's list - let's put it to work.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 2 - READABLE AT A GLANCE (~35s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Scroll the list slowly; let the colors do the talking. ]

Start with what the list tells you before you touch it. The colored border
and dot are PRIORITY - red High, orange Medium, green Low. The date chip is
URGENCY - red for overdue like "Prep quarterly numbers," amber for due today
like "Call Mom." The solid pill is the CATEGORY, the grey badge is SIZE.

One glance and Michael knows the red, overdue work is where his attention
goes - and the "Update LinkedIn to World's Best Boss" task can wait.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 3 - SMART SORT: WHAT TO DO NEXT (~45s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Sort dropdown -> "Smart (auto)". Point at the new top of the
list. ]

SMART sort turns that instinct into the default - and it's the heart of the
app.

Instead of sorting on one field, it scores every task by blending urgency,
priority, and a quick-win boost for smaller tasks, then floats the
important-and-urgent ones to the top. Watch: the overdue quarterly numbers
and the past-due car registration rise up; the Dundie Awards and calling Mom
sit right behind; the low-priority, no-deadline stuff sinks to the bottom on
its own.

You open the app and the top of the list IS your answer to "what now?" - no
triage required.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 4 - AWAITING: HIDE WHAT YOU CAN'T ACT ON (~35s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Scroll to the Awaiting section; point at the two purple
hourglass tasks. ]

Focus is also about hiding what you CAN'T act on. That's AWAITING - pure GTD
"waiting-on."

Michael's waiting on a promo rate to book Jamaica, and on HR to transfer
Toby - those live down here, out of his active list.

[ ON SCREEN: On an active task, click the hourglass to defer with a note +
bring-back date. ]

Deferring takes one click: you note what you're waiting on and a bring-back
date, and on that date it AUTOMATICALLY returns to the active list. You
never have to remember to check back.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 5 - IDEA PIPELINE: KEEP "SOMEDAY" ALIVE (~40s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Scroll to the Idea Pipeline section; point at the three teal
idea rows. ]

Not everything is a task yet. The IDEA PIPELINE is your "someday/maybe"
bucket - ideas with NO due date and NO done state, so they never clutter
what's actionable.

It's where Michael parks the big swings: a family-friendly restaurant chain,
Michael Scott Paper Company 2.0, and his book, "Somehow I Manage."

[ ON SCREEN: Click "+ Add idea"; then hover a task and click the lightbulb
to park it; then the return arrow on an idea to promote it. ]

Capture one with "+ Add idea," or hover any task and hit the lightbulb to
park it for later. When an idea's time comes, one click promotes it back to
the to-do list. Your ambitions stay captured without drowning today's work.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 6 - FRICTIONLESS CAPTURE (~20s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Quickly: + to add, click a row to edit/complete/delete, hover
for the duplicate icon. Fast montage. ]

Getting things IN stays effortless. The plus button captures a task with all
its fields; clicking a row edits, completes, or deletes it; hovering
duplicates a repeat. Search and filters shrink a long list in a keystroke.
It just stays out of your way so the framework can do the work.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 7 - YOUR DATA, YOUR FILES (~25s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Open the menu; point at auto-save status, Switch folder, Export
backup (.zip). ]

And you OWN all of it. GSD stores your tasks as plain JSON files in a folder
you pick - that folder IS the database, and every change auto-saves there
instantly. No cloud, no lock-in.

Back up to a zip in one click, move the folder to another machine, even diff
it in git. It's your list, in your files, forever.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 8 - WRAP-UP (~20s)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ON SCREEN: Return to the list with Smart sort on. ]

One offline file, your data, and a list that TELLS you what to do next:
priority and urgency at a glance, Smart sort surfacing what matters,
Awaiting holding what you can't act on, and the Idea Pipeline keeping someday
alive without crowding today.

Get it out of your head. Let GSD tell you what's next. That's getting stuff
done.

[ ON SCREEN: Fade out. ]
